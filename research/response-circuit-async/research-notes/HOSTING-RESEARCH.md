## Actual source and mathematical scope

OpenAI MatrixMultiplication/Recovery/FiniteSchedule.lean was read in full through its finite_schedule_yield proof. Its lotTick, stageActive_iff_unique_lot and sum_tick_minima make boundary work explicit. FieldConstruction/ScheduledYield links abstract capacity to physical order. Their exact source hashes are in ARCHITECTURE-SOURCES.json. They motivated the finite boundary-stock screen; that screen did not produce the final gain. No Lean theorem was directly instantiated or newly certified.

An exact fixed-support donor-basis invariant was also derived. If every donor is used, every kernel participant pays its entrance-to-first-frame increment and each donor pays one entrance increment. Exchanging donors and pivots at fixed participating support therefore leaves the recursive histogram unchanged. All eleven baseline classes have every donor used; first-frame dimensions are {2:80, 3:104, 6:28}. DONOR-BASIS-LEDGER.json records the exact check, and DONOR-AND-ALLOCATION.md states the sufficient pricing identity. This directed the root search toward changed supports instead of redundant basis swaps.

PR520's open allocator and delivery-layer map were read. Its Hall-cut formulation agrees with the need to turn aggregate counts into actual port assignments. No PR520 code was imported: this round used the project's existing finite pattern-union optimizer and literal matching reconstruction. Neither those mechanisms nor allocation-aware kernel selection are claimed globally new. The contribution is the frozen new source choice, its literal allocation and the independently checked resulting bound.

The native optimization runs are discovery tools only. The final verifier consumes frozen sources and witnesses, runs no optimizer, and retains all inherited all-size compiler and analytic-transfer assumptions. No process from this hosting lane remains running.
